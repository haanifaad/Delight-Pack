# Delight Pack - ERP, CRM, & Smart Ecosystem

## Overview
This repository contains the comprehensive digital ecosystem for **Delight Pack**, a premium packaging company. The project envisions an integrated platform that connects a public-facing website, a customer portal, an admin dashboard, employee management, inventory tracking, finance, marketing, and advanced AI automation. 

Our ultimate goal is to reduce manual work, increase operational efficiency, elevate our brand presence, and build deep customer trust through a fully automated, data-driven Smart Factory model.

## Core Modules

### 1. Public Website 🌐
A professional, conversion-focused website featuring:
- Hero animations, company introductions, and client testimonials.
- A comprehensive **Products Page** (Food, Box, Industrial packaging).
- A **Services Page** (Custom design, bulk packaging, delivery).
- Integrated SEO, news/blog sections, and a dynamic Contact Page (WhatsApp, Google Maps).

### 2. Customer Portal 👥
A dedicated space where clients can:
- Place and track orders.
- Download invoices and view quotations.
- Request custom packaging and chat with support.

### 3. Admin Dashboard 📊
The central control center providing:
- Real-time statistics (income, expenses, profit margins).
- Granular analytics (seasonal demand, customer trends).
- Order control and automated notifications (low stock, delayed delivery).

### 4. Employee Management 👨‍🏭
Streamlined workforce tools:
- Attendance tracking (QR/Face recognition options).
- Task assignment and shift planning.
- Salary and performance management.

### 5. Inventory & Stock System 📦
Critical tracking for a packaging operation:
- Auto stock deductions and low-stock warnings.
- Tracking of raw materials, printed boxes, rolls, ink, and machine parts.
- QR and barcode scanning for easy inventory checks.

### 6. Finance System 💰
Comprehensive financial overview:
- Income and expense tracking.
- Monthly profit analysis and growth comparison.
- Automated GST/VAT invoice generation.

### 7. Marketing & Publicity 📢
Integrated tools to outpace competitors:
- Automated social media posting and advertising campaigns.
- SEO analytics, blog publishing, and customer review tracking.
- WhatsApp marketing and digital catalog generation.

### 8. AI Automation 🤖
The strongest weapon for scaling:
- **AI Assistant:** Instant replies to customer queries.
- **Quote Generator:** Auto-estimates for material, printing, and delivery costs.
- **AI Prediction:** Forecasts future demand and stock shortages.
- **Complaint Analyzer:** Detects frequent issues from customer feedback.

### 9. Mobile Apps 📱
Dedicated applications to stay connected:
- **Customer App:** Order tracking and support chat.
- **Admin App:** Live analytics and employee monitoring.

### 10. Future Smart Factory 🏭
IoT Integration (ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi) to monitor:
- Machine temperature, electricity usage, and production count.

## System Architecture 💻
- **Frontend:** React, Next.js
- **Mobile:** Flutter
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Cloud:** Firebase / AWS
- **AI:** OpenAI API, Gemini API
- **Charts:** Chart.js, Recharts

## Special Features 🔥
- **Multi-language Support:** English, Malayalam, Arabic.
- **Dark Mode & Offline Support**
- **QR Code Product Tracking**
- **WhatsApp Order Bot & Automated SMS/Email**

## Final Vision 🚀
*ERP + CRM + AI + Branding + Marketing + Smart Factory*

This system transforms traditional packaging operations into a modern, highly efficient, and data-driven ecosystem.
